# Research and data references

1. Prasad, A. & Chandra, S. (2024). PhiUSIIL Phishing URL (Website). UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/967/phiusiil+phishing+url+dataset — CC BY 4.0.
2. Gorishniy, Y., Kotelnikov, A., & Babenko, A. (2025). *TabM: Advancing Tabular Deep Learning with Parameter-Efficient Ensembling*. ICLR 2025. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/c1ba41c694834aeef91ae161711d4939-Abstract-Conference.html ; official code https://github.com/yandex-research/tabm . Our optional `tabm_lite.py` is a **small BatchEnsemble-inspired adaptation**, not official TabM or a paper reproduction.
3. *Rethinking Phishing Detection: How Dataset Quality Affects Model Generalization* (ACIT 2025), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ACIT65614.2025.11185656. Motivates deduplication, domain-aware evaluation and cross-dataset caution.
4. *Explainable Comprehensive Analysis of Cross-Dataset Phishing Detection Models* (QPAIN 2026), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/QPAIN69676.2026.11546228. Motivates realistic generalization evaluation and interpretable lexical features.

Do not interpret any project smoke-test scores as claims from these papers.
