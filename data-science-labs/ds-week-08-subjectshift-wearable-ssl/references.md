# Research and data references

1. Reyes-Ortiz et al. (2013). Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphones, UCI. https://doi.org/10.24432/C54S4K (CC BY 4.0).
2. Cai et al. (2026-05-08). BenchHAR: Benchmarking Self-Supervised Learning for Generalizable Sensor-based Activity Recognition. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08296 — 258K samples, unseen-distribution SSL evaluation; this project does not reproduce their experiments.
3. Guo & Nakayama (2025). Towards User-Generalizable Wearable-Sensor-Based Human Activity Recognition: A Multi-Task Contrastive Learning Approach. Sensors 25(22), 6988. https://doi.org/10.3390/s25226988 — subject-aware evaluation motivation.
4. Dynamics-Guided Cross-Modality Masked State Space Modeling for Robust Wearable Human Activity Recognition (IEEE AIC, October 2026 indexing). https://doi.org/10.1109/AIC70896.2026.11717057 — inspiration for motion-aware masking and cross-channel reconstruction; **our CNN is a simplified adaptation, not an SSM reproduction**.
5. Cai et al. (2025). Towards Generalizable Human Activity Recognition: A Survey. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12213 — reviews user, device, environment domain shifts.

Paper results must never be conflated with this project's independently measured metrics.
